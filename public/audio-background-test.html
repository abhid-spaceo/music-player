Background audio test

Throwaway page. Not part of the app. Proves whether a plain <audio> element keeps playing when the phone is locked or backgrounded.

What to do
  1. Tap Play. Wait until you hear music.
  2. Press the power key to lock the screen. Music should keep playing.
  3. Look at the lock screen — you should see the title, artist, artwork and working play / pause / next / prev.
  4. Unlock, come back here, and read the log below.
  5. Repeat with the home key instead, and by switching to another app for 2+ minutes.
  6. Tap Jump to last 15s, lock the screen, and wait — this proves the queue can advance to the next track with no finger on the screen.
